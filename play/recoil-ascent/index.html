Recoil Ascent

A 2D physics platformer I wrote in Java with LibGDX for COMP 2522. Your gun's recoil is how you move: aim away from where you want to go, fire, and climb to the top as fast as you can.

Controls

Start
Enter
Walk
A D or the left and right arrows
Jump
Space
Aim
W A S D or the arrows, in 8 directions
Fire
Shift or J

Click the game first so it receives your keys. It needs a keyboard, so it will not play on a phone.

This is the original Java game compiled to JavaScript with TeaVM. Your best times are kept in this browser only.

Art: Whimsy Hallow character and tileset, and Ranitaya's guns pack.
